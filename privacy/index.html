Privacy Policy.

How Meria Studio handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

1. Who we are

Meria Studio is an independent web design studio based in Belfast, Northern Ireland. You can contact us at hello@meriastudio.com.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us through our enquiry form or by email, we may receive your name, email address, business name, website or social media link, details of the service you are interested in, and any other information you choose to include in your message.

3. Why we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, prepare website concepts or quotes, discuss potential projects, deliver agreed services and keep appropriate business records. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Contact form provider

Our website contact form is processed using Formspree. Information submitted through the form is therefore handled through Formspree's service as well as being delivered to Meria Studio. You should review Formspree's own privacy information if you want details of how it processes submissions.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The website currently uses browser local storage to remember whether you have dismissed the cookie notice. We do not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural tracking on this site. If analytics or other non-essential tracking is added later, this policy and the consent mechanism should be updated before those tools are enabled.

6. Links to other websites

Our site contains links to third-party websites and social platforms, including Instagram and portfolio demo sites. Those services have their own privacy policies and are responsible for their own data practices.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for communication, project delivery, legal or accounting requirements, and legitimate business records.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights over your personal information, including rights to access, correct, erase or restrict its use. To make a request, contact us at hello@meriastudio.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@meriastudio.com.
