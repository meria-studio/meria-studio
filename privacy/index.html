Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

A clear overview of the information we collect through enquiries, how we use it, and your rights.

Last updated: June 2026

1. About this policy

Meria Studio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you provide through this website.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us through our website, we may collect your name, email address, business name and any information you include in your message.

3. How we use your information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide quotations, communicate with potential and existing clients, and improve our website and services.

4. Contact forms

Our contact form may be processed through a trusted third-party form provider. Information submitted through the form is used only to respond to your enquiry or provide requested services.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember cookie preferences and understand how the website is used.

6. Analytics and third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services such as website hosting providers, contact form providers and analytics tools. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

8. How long we keep information

We only keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, maintain records or meet reasonable business requirements.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@meriastudio.com or message us on Instagram at @meria.studios.
